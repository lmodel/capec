# References

* [CAPEC catalogue](capec-catalogue.md) - The Common Attack Pattern Enumeration and Classification, the catalogue of attack patterns that The MITRE Corporation publishes as XML. Version 3.9 of 24 January 2023 is the latest, with 615 attack patterns, 78 categories, 13 views and 440 external references.
* [CAPEC XSD](capec-xsd.md) - The XML Schema of the CAPEC catalogue, version 3.5, namespace `http://capec.mitre.org/capec-3`. Its own header says 3.5.0, dated 21 October 2021. The schema's keys and listed values are this file's.
* [CAPEC Terms of Use](capec-terms-of-use.md) - MITRE's terms for CAPEC: a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence for research, development and commercial use, under which a copy must reproduce MITRE's copyright designation and the licence. NOTICE and `upstream/TERMS.txt` do so.
