# Attack pattern

A catalogued way that adversaries go about an attack, with what it takes and how to resist it.[^capec_entries.yaml]

It has a CAPEC number, a name, an abstraction level and a status, and may hold prerequisites, skills, consequences and mitigations.[^capec_entries.yaml]
