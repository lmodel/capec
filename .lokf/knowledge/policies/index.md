# Policies

* [AI covenant](ai-covenant.md) - The rules for AI-assisted work here: whoever submits owns the contribution, an element says what the XSD says, a rule states only what the upstream states, a mapping is read at its source, and no commit carries an AI co-authorship trailer.
* [Licence and attribution](license-and-attribution.md) - The repository's licences and what it takes from elsewhere: Apache-2.0 for the schema and code, CC BY 4.0 for the bundle, and MITRE's CAPEC Terms of Use for the XSD and catalogue, whose designation and licence NOTICE reproduces. No endorsement is implied.
* [Security policy](security-policy.md) - How to report a vulnerability in this repository, by GitHub's private reporting, what executes here and what holds each surface. A problem in CAPEC itself goes to the CAPEC team.
