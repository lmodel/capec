# AI covenant

A contributor owns what they submit, whatever tools helped.[^ai_covenant.md]

Four rules are particular to this repository:[^ai_covenant.md]

- an element says what the XSD says;
- a rule states only what the upstream states;
- a mapping is read at its source first;
- descriptions are this project's own words.

AI co-authorship in commit messages is discouraged, and a scheduled agent's change always goes through a person's review.[^ai_covenant.md]
